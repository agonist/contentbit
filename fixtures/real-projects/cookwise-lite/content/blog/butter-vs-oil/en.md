# Butter vs oil

## Overview

Butter brings dairy flavor and browning, while oil usually gives a higher heat
ceiling. When a recipe moves into hard searing, check the [oil smoke point guide](https://fond.kitchen/blog/cooking-oil-smoke-points/)
and choose a stable oil such as [olive oil](https://fond.kitchen/glossary/olive-oil/)
when the method fits.

:::callout{type="tip" title="Pan sauce habit"}
Use oil for the sear, then finish with butter after the pan temperature drops.
:::
