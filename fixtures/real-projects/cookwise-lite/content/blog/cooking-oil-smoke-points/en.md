# Cooking oil smoke points

## Overview

Oil choice starts with heat. A saute pan, fryer, and roasting tray all ask for
different fat stability, so the smoke point is the fastest way to avoid bitter
flavors. Compare this guide with [butter vs oil](https://fond.kitchen/blog/butter-vs-oil/)
and the [olive oil](https://fond.kitchen/glossary/olive-oil/) glossary entry.

:::key-metrics
- 520F | Refined avocado oil ceiling
- 375F | Typical extra virgin olive oil ceiling
:::
