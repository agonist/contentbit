# Olive oil

## Why it matters

Olive oil can be a finishing fat, a sauteing fat, or a moderate-heat cooking
fat depending on refinement. The safest way to choose the right bottle is to
check the [smoke point guide](https://fond.kitchen/blog/cooking-oil-smoke-points/)
and match the oil to the heat.

:::callout{type="note" title="Refinement changes use"}
Extra virgin olive oil favors flavor. Refined olive oil favors higher heat.
:::
