# Dialing in espresso

Use this espresso dial in guide as a repeatable extraction workflow: change one
variable, taste, and adjust. It starts the loop and points to the grinder notes
and shot log that keep each change honest.

:::callout{type="tldr"}
Start at **1:2 in 28 seconds**. Sour means grind finer, bitter means grind
coarser. Change one thing at a time.
:::

## The numbers that matter

:::key-metrics
- 18g | Dose in
- 36g | Yield out
- 28s | Shot time
- 93°C | Brew temp
:::

Those four numbers describe almost everything about a shot. Hit them and you
are in the neighborhood; the last 10% is taste.

## The dial-in loop

:::steps
1. Pull a shot at your current setting and **taste it** before judging the numbers.
2. Sour or salty? Grind *finer*. Bitter or hollow? Grind *coarser*.
3. Adjust only the grind until the shot runs `1:2` in 25–30 seconds.
4. Lock the recipe and repeat it twice to confirm it was not a fluke.
:::

:::callout{type="warning" title="One variable at a time"}
Changing dose, grind, and temperature together tells you nothing. You will
not know which change did what.
:::

## On the bench

:::quick-ref
- Dose scale | 0.1g resolution
- Distribution | WDT tool, 0.4mm needles
- Tamp | 30lb, level beats hard
- Basket | 18g precision
:::

:::quote{author="James Hoffmann" role="The World Atlas of Coffee"}
Great espresso is not a recipe. It is a process of paying attention.
:::
