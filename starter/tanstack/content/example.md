# Dialing in espresso

Espresso is a feedback loop: you change one variable, taste, and adjust. This
guide walks the loop with every Content Block along the way — the same
Markdown renders in any framework contentbit supports.

:::callout{type="tldr"}
Start at **1:2 in 28 seconds**. Sour means grind finer, bitter means grind
coarser. Change one thing at a time.
:::

## The numbers that matter

:::key-metrics
- 18g | Dose in
- 36g | Yield out
- 28s | Shot time
- 93°C | Brew temp
:::

Those four numbers describe almost everything about a shot. Hit them and you
are in the neighborhood; the last 10% is taste.

## The dial-in loop

:::steps
1. Pull a shot at your current setting and **taste it** before judging the numbers.
2. Sour or salty? Grind *finer*. Bitter or hollow? Grind *coarser*.
3. Adjust only the grind until the shot runs `1:2` in 25–30 seconds.
4. Lock the recipe and repeat it twice to confirm it was not a fluke.
:::

:::callout{type="warning" title="One variable at a time"}
Changing dose, grind, and temperature together tells you nothing. You will
not know which change did what.
:::

## Grinder settings

Every grinder speaks its own dialect. Starting points:

:::tabs
::tab{title="Flat burr"}
Start mid-range and move in **single steps**. Flat burrs are unforgiving but
consistent — small moves, big differences.
::tab{title="Conical burr"}
Start two-thirds toward fine. Conicals are gentler near the edges, so you can
move in *double steps* until you get close.
::tab{title="Hand grinder"}
Count clicks from zero. Most hand grinders land between 9 and 14 clicks for
espresso. Write your number down.
:::

## Questions everyone asks

:::faq
::faq-item{question="Do I really need a scale?"}
Yes. Eyeballing dose swings ±2g, which is the difference between sour and
bitter at the same grind setting.
::faq-item{question="How fresh should the beans be?"}
Use beans roasted 7–30 days ago. Fresher than a week traps CO₂ and crema
lies to you; older than a month and sweetness fades.
::faq-item{question="Why does my time drift day to day?"}
Humidity and bean age change extraction. A 2–3 second drift is normal —
chase taste, not the clock.
:::

That is the whole game: small changes, honest tasting, and notes you trust.
